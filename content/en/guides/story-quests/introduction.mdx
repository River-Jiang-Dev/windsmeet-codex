## Overview

Where Winds Meet unfolds against the backdrop of the Five Dynasties period (907–960 CE) — one of the most turbulent eras in Chinese history.

## The Main Story Arc

The story begins when your character arrives in Yunzhou, a city caught between rival factions.

## First Quests

1. **The Merchant's Request** — Talk to Elder Shen at the market
2. **A Stranger's Blade** — Follow the trail to the river district
