## Origins

The Jade Blade style depicted in Where Winds Meet draws heavily from the Wudang soft-sword tradition, which emerged during the late Tang and early Five Dynasties period.

## Historical Context

During the Five Dynasties period (907–960 CE), China fragmented into multiple competing states. Martial artists who had served Tang dynasty courts found themselves stateless.

## In the Game

The game's version of this school is led by Master Qiu, whose family sword was passed down through seven generations of conflict.
